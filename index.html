Project

College Management System

A web-based system to manage student, faculty and academic records in one central database. It covers attendance, fees, results, timetable and notices, with separate logins for Admin, Faculty and Students.

My contribution: Prepared the project presentation and explained the project workflow and features.

Read project report

Problem

Many colleges manage student records, attendance, fees, results and notices by hand or in separate spreadsheets. This causes duplicate data, errors, delays and extra work for staff and teachers.

Objectives

  • Keep student, faculty and course records in one database
  • Automate attendance and report generation
  • Manage fee details, payment status and receipts
  • Store and show exam marks and results
  • Role-based login for Admin, Faculty and Students
  • Reduce paperwork and manual errors
